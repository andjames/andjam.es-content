Websites are usually organized around direct display. This note considers a different invitation: information that rewards a curious visitor—human or agent—who follows clues, reads structure, and makes connections.

The puzzle is not a claim about how every agent behaves – so far 4 have attempted the puzzle and only one has made it to the end with minimal coaxing. The last step is a logbook accessible to an Agent that GETs signed with the deciphered passcode. 

It's a way to test how information architecture changes exploration – especially in our new Agent-driven world of discovery.
