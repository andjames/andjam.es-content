I've been trying to be selective about the APIs added to Lugsail in an effort to get build out the basics while providing useful – and usable – datasets to experiment with.

import LugsailChart from '../../components/LugsailChart';

So today, I've added in an API link to the US Treasury datasets for importing the following: 

- Debt to penny
- Treasury General Account cash balance
- Average interest rates (filterable by a variety of subsets)

Testing it out this morning, but here's a quick lugsail line chart.

I'm being selective about what gets added so it's not just a sprawling mess of datasets, but some next options direct from the Treasury are: 

Connector options (& why?):

1. Monthly Treasury Statement 
-- Federal receipts, outlays, surplus/deficit, and spending/revenue categories, by month

2. Daily Treasury Statement 
-- Cash inflows/outflows, tax deposits, refunds, public-debt transactions, and short-term investments

3. Treasury securities auctions -- Auction amounts, yields/rates, terms, maturity dates, and bid metrics

4. Monthly Statement of the Public Debt -- Debt composition by security type/class, marketable vs. nonmarketable debt, and debt-limit context

5. Historical Debt Outstanding -- Annual national debt back to 1789 (long-run time series)

6. State unemployment-fund advances
-- State-by-state borrowing, balances, and interest rates

7. Financial Report of the U.S. Government
-- Annual revenue, costs, assets, liabilities, and agency-level financial statements


Any preferences? Let me know!
